Today we'll be reading "Pete the Cat Big Easter Adventure" by Kimberly and James Dean. Pete was excited! Easter was here! He couldn't wait for his basket of goodies. Jelly beans were his favorite. Uh, oh! His basket was empty except for a note. "Pete, Please help! Find the eggs. Paint the eggs. Hide the eggs. Thanks! The Easter Bunny P.S. Wear these!" Pete put on the bunny ears and thought, "A cat with ears like a bunny—now that's funny." "Happy Easter, chickens! Do you have any eggs today?" Pete asked. "I am helping the Easter Bunny." "Sure, Pete. We have a lot of eggs," the chickens said. How many eggs do you see there? Let's count them—one, two, three! "We are happy to help, but don't you need a bunny nose and fluffy bunny tail?" The chickens were right. A bunny nose and tail would be neat. Then Pete's costume would be complete! What's in the bottle Pete's holding? Let's read it—"GLUE." What's in that box on the table? "COTTON." Pete put on the nose and tail like a bunny's. "A cat dressed up like a bunny—now that's funny!" Now Pete was ready. It was getting late, and he still had a lot of eggs to decorate! What colors would Pete use? What's Pete thinking about? A rainbow! Hop! Hop! Hop! Off to the toolshed for paint and brushes. Let's read what's inside these boxes—"NAILS, SCREWS" Pete couldn't wait to paint the eggs! What colors do you see? Say them with me—RED, GREEN, YELLOW. Some eggs had one color. Some eggs had two. Some eggs were red, and some eggs were blue! When the egg-painting was done, Pete had a basket full of bright, colorful, amazing eggs! Now hiding them would be lots of fun. What colors does Pete have? Say them with me: ORANGE, YELLOW, RED, GREEN, BLUE But where, oh where, would Pete hide the eggs? Around the neighborhood— for all his friends to find! Pete hid eggs in flower pots. He hid them in the water spout. And when he was done hiding the eggs, Pete the Cat was all worn out! "Helping others out is what Easter is all about," Pete said. Pete's job was done. He was hiding the last one ... when the Easter Bunny arrived! "Great job, Pete! You were a big help," said the Easter Bunny. The Easter Bunny gave Pete an award for a job well done. What does it say on the award? "Number One Helper!" Helping others can be lots of fun!
