The Velveteen Rabbit, or How Toys Became Real, by&nbsp; Margery Williams, illustrated by Gennady Spirin There was once a Velveteen Rabbit, and&nbsp; in the beginning he was really splendid.&nbsp;&nbsp; He was fat and bunchy, as a rabbit should be;&nbsp; his coat was spotted brown and white, he had real&nbsp;&nbsp; thread whiskers, and his ears were lined with pink&nbsp; sateen.
