Here are three things I notice about this story:&nbsp; First, I notice the distinctive style of the&nbsp; illustrations. They look as though they are&nbsp;&nbsp; made out of stained glass, like you would find&nbsp; in the windows of a church. This gives the story&nbsp;&nbsp; an extra feeling of importance,&nbsp; as if these are holy characters&nbsp;&nbsp; taking part in events that are blessed. It&nbsp; was a very smart choice by the illustrator.&nbsp; Next, at the climax of the story, I found myself&nbsp; filled with emotion when Merlin stepped forward&nbsp;&nbsp; and revealed that Arthur has a special destiny. I&nbsp; have always been drawn to moments like this, when&nbsp;&nbsp; characters come to understand the purpose of their&nbsp; lives--that they are meant to accomplish something&nbsp;&nbsp; important. As a boy, I imagined that kind of&nbsp; thing happening to me. Even now, as an adult,&nbsp;&nbsp; I am having a hard time letting go of that.
