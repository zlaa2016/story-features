There was once a bright yellow bus who spent her days driving. Every morning they climbed in. Pitterpatter, pitterpatter, giggle, giggle, patter. The yellow bus carried them from one important place to another, and they filled her with joy. One day, a new driver came and drove the yellow bus along unfamiliar roads. Every morning they climbed in. Shuffle clunk. Shuffle clunk. Greek creek clunk. The yellow bus carried them from one important place to another. And they filled her with joy. After a long time, someone new came and drove the yellow bus into the city. She parked her in a quiet spot, walked away, and never returned. Nobody climbed in. The yellow bus didn't drive from one important place to another. No pitterpatters, no giggles, no shuffles, creeks, or clunks. The yellow bus was empty until one cold night they climbed in. Rustle bump, rustle bump, brump. And huddled together. The yellow bus didn't carry them from one important place to another. Yet every night, with hush noises and soft voices, they climbed in, and they filled her with joy. Early one morning, a truck arrived and tow the yellow bus through the city, across a bridge, into the country, along rolling hills, and down onto a farm nestled deep in a valley. The truck driver parked her at the bottom of a field on the bank of a river and drove away. But just as the yellow bus was missing the pitterpatters, giggles, shuffles, clunks, rustles, and bumps. They climbed in. Clipclop clip clop mclop. The yellow bus didn't carry them from one important place to another, and still they filled her with joy. From her spot in the field on the farm in the valley, below rolling hills and beside the river, the yellow bus could hear booming noises nearby. It reminded her of the sounds of the city, a lot of large trucks and heavy machinery. Then all at once, the loud noises stopped. Soon the farmer came and gathered them together and took them away. No more clips and clops and mess. The farm was quiet and so was a yellow bus. Gradually from around the bend, the river began to swell and rise and rise and rise and rise. The yellow bus was alone until they swam in. Swish, swish, swish, swish, glo. The yellow bus didn't carry them from one important place to another.
