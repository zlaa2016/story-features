Milo lived in a neat little house on a neat little street. He loved his home for lots of reasons, but mainly because his best friend Jay lived next door. Milo and Jay shared the good times, the bad times, and everything in between. Nothing could beat the feeling of having a best friend. Then one day, a new family moved into the big old house across the road. Milo watched as a girl ran excitedly around the garden. She smiled and waved. Milo waved back. Later that afternoon, Milo went to call for Jay, but he wasn't in. He had gone over to the big old house across the road without telling Milo. Jay and the girl were in the garden, laughing very loudly. A funny feeling squirmed around in Milo's tummy. "Hi Milo," called Jay. "This is Suzie. Isn't her garden amazing?" Milo nodded, but the jealous, squirmy feeling was nothing to do with Suzie's massive garden and everything to do with how much fun Jay was having without him. The next day, Jay was out again. Milo knew exactly where he would be, and sure enough, he was outside reading comics with Suzie. Then Milo had a horrible thought: what if Jay didn't want to be best friends with him anymore? The squirmy feeling took over completely, and as it did, something very strange happened. A green-eyed monster popped up right beside him. "It's not fair," muttered the monster. "Jay is your friend, not Suzie's." From then on, the green-eyed monster wouldn't leave him alone. Whenever he saw Jay and Suzie laughing, the green-eyed monster said they were making fun of him. Whenever he saw them playing, the monster hissed that they were having more fun without him. Every time Milo saw Suzie and Jay together, the green-eyed monster made him feel terrible. So Milo decided to stop seeing them at all. If they called around at his house, he pretended not to be in. If he saw them at the park, he would hide. If either of them asked what was wrong, he just said "nothing" and turned away. Day after day, he walked around with only the miserable mutterings of the green-eyed monster for company. Until one day, Suzie tapped him on the shoulder. "Why aren't you and Jay friends anymore?" she asked. "He really misses you." She looked very upset. The monster said it was a trick, that Suzie was lying, but Milo shook his head. It was time to get rid of that pesky monster once and for all. Milo shut his eyes tight, took a deep breath, and tried to force the bad feeling away. The green-eyed monster protested and muttered mean words, but his voice became quieter and quieter until at last, it was completely gone. Finally, Milo could see the truth. The green-eyed monster had nearly ruined everything. Now he did the only thing he could: he said, "I'm sorry." And he really was. Soon they were all friends again, laughing and playing in Suzie's garden together. Milo smiled. Nothing could beat the feeling of having a best friend, except having two of them. Good for you, Milo! You got rid of that green-eyed monster and gained a new friend. Listen, it doesn't feel nice to be jealous, but it does happen to everyone. It's much better to try, no matter how hard, to dig deep and even pretend if you have to, to be happy for a friend. You never know what might happen. They might not even realise that you have such big feelings. I'll be back to read you another bedtime story very soon. Till then, you should go to bed. Night night!
