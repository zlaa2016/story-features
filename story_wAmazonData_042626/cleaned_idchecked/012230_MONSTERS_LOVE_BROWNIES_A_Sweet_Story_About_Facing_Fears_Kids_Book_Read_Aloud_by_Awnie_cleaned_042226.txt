Monsters Love Brownies. Written by Ben Okan. Illustrated by Kamal Sharma. I bet you thought that cookies were a monster's favorite treat because some hungry monsters munch their cookies on the street. Surprisingly, most monsters, though, prefer a different snack. They scarf down brownies by the pound. They eat the tallest stack. They gobble gooey centers and they crunch on crispy sides. They swirl in peanut fudge and whisk in walnuts open wide. The monsters with two heads prefer a double chocolate chip. And the ones with big old claws pour sticky syrup for the grip. As much as they love brownies, well, they love one thing much more. They love a game of hide and seek. They'll play it while you snore. They squeeze under your bed and wait to give you quite a scare. They'll hide out in your wardrobe if you dare to enter there. Their love for hide and seek is why those brownies are the key. They only hide out late at night. Dark foods are hard to see. They need to hide for hours to catch young children by surprise. And having dark, delicious treats won't ruin their surprise. Are you scared? You shouldn't be. These monsters are so sweet. I know their fangs are long, but they are just for biting treats. Those growls, they're from their tums. It makes them hungry playing tricks. They love to scare you, sure, but all those scares are just for kicks. Hey kid, you baked some brownies for the monsters hide and seek. These brownies are enormous. They will make those monsters shriek. You're scared, but you just found the key to keeping them in sight. I bet they'll let you join them in their hideandseek tonight. Wait, friend. I must make sure. I hope you left your milk downstairs cuz milk will make those monsters mad. They'll give you eerie glares. While you might think that milk goes well with brownies on the side, remember milk is white and lighter foods are hard to hide. You brought some milk. Uh-oh. Not good. Let's hide it quickly, please. Let's stash it in this chest. It's way too small for them to squeeze. Okay, the milk is gone. Let's hide behind this door. Oh no, I haven't seen that tiny monster here before. A tiny chest. No chance that kid expects to find me here. What's that? I'm dripping wet. It's milk. My greatest fear. Quickly, wipe it off before that sleepy kid awakes. Here's a brownie. Now, calm down. You've still got milky shakes. Um, guys, you won't believe this, but I think you'll like this milk. It's white, but so refreshing, golden, creamy, smooth as silk. You're right. It makes our brownies even more delicious. How? I think it's time we played our hide and seek games near a cow. My friend, you helped the monsters overcome their milk alarms. Their lives are changed. They move their hideandsek games to the barns. Now that their mouths are white, they'll never want to hide in gloom. That's why you'll never see a monster in your room. Wow, friends, you did it again. You helped the monsters face their fear of milk, and now they can enjoy their brownies without a worry.
