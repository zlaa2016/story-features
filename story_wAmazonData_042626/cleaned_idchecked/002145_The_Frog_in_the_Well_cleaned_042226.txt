In the middle of a quiet forest in Oregon, surrounded by pine trees and wild flowers, lived a small frog named Finn. Finn lived at the bottom of an old stone well. He loved it there. The water was cool, the bugs were tasty, and he had a perfect circle of sky to look at. One breezy afternoon, as Finn was lounging on a lily pad, he heard a plop. A large sea turtle named Shelly peaked over the edge of the well. Finn blinked. Ocean? What's that? A bigger puddle. Shel smiled. That night, Finn couldn't sleep. He stared up at the round bit of sky above him. Finn climbed up the rough stones, his webbed feet slipping here and there. With one last push, "Hop!" he landed on the soft moss outside. The world took his breath away. Finn followed the sound of the river, meeting all sorts of new friends. A busy beaver building a dam, a family of ducks, and even a curious raccoon who offered him a shiny pebble. Each animal told him about the ocean, where water stretched farther than the forest itself. Finn's heart filled with wonder. I have to see it," he said. After many days of hopping through meadows and across streams, Finn reached a sandy shore. He froze. Finn stayed by the shore for a while, watching seagulls glide and crabs scuttle across the sand. He realized how small his well had been and how much he had yet to see. When Finn finally returned to his well, the other frogs were waiting. They croked. "You've been gone for weeks. What did you see?" Finn smiled. "I saw forests, rivers, and an ocean bigger than a thousand wells." Some frogs laughed and said, "No way." He knew his well was a wonderful home. But it wasn't the only home. The world was full of amazing places waiting to be discovered. And as the morning sun warmed the stones, another small frog filled with dreams of the ocean began to climb. For the sky, Finn had learned, was not a ceiling, but a doorway.
