Once upon a time in a far away land, there lived a beautiful princess named Snow White. Now, Snow White wasn't your typical princess. While other princesses spent their days getting ready for royal balls, practicing their curtsies, and learning how to be the perfect little princess, Snow White spent her days wishing she could be just an ordinary girl. She longed to run through the forest, climb trees, and eat yummy snacks without having to worry about what the queen or the royal court would think of her. One day, Snow White was strolling through the castle gardens when she met a little bird who told her that all the woodland creatures were having a picnic in the forest. The little bird had been watching Snow White from afar and had fallen in love with her kind heart and gentle ways. "I wish I could join you," Snow White sighed, gazing at the forest. But the little bird chirped, "Why don't you just run away from the castle and live in the forest with us? You can be our princess." And so Snow White did. She packed a bag with some bread and cheese, then ran away from the castle and into the forest. There, in a clearing, she found the most beautiful cottage made of twigs and leaves. And when she stepped inside, she saw the cutest little table and chairs made of acorns and mushrooms. Just then, a family of deer approached her and invited her to be their guest. They showed her to a bedroom where the walls were covered with flowers and the bed was made of soft moss. As the nights wore on, Snow White told the deer family all about her adventures in the castle, and they told her about life in the forest. Before long, it was time for bed. And as Snow White laid down on her bed of moss, she couldn't help but feel happy. For the first time in her life, she felt like she truly belonged somewhere. The next morning, Snow White awoke to the sound of birds singing and deer playing. She spent the day exploring the forest with her new friends, and they all had so much fun. As the sun began to set, Snow White knew it was time to return to the castle. She said her goodbyes to her new friends and promised to visit again soon. Back at the castle, Snow White found that she couldn't stop smiling. She realized that while being a princess wasn't all that bad, being a regular girl who could do whatever she wanted was even better. From that day on, whenever Snow White felt like the life of a princess was getting to be too much, she would escape to the forest and stay with her friends, the deer, and they all lived happily ever After.
