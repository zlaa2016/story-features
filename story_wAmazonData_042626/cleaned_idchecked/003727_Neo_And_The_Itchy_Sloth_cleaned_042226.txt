In the quiet green heart of the rainforest, Neo wandered beneath the whispering trees where fireflies glowed and the air smelled of rain. Then he heard it. Scratch, scratch, scratch. It was coming from up in the trees. Oh," laughed Neo. "You're itchy." The sloth blinked slowly and gave a shy little smile. "Too itchy to sleep?" asked Neo. The sloth nodded slowly. "Exactly, my fur. Maybe I can help, said Neo. He peered at the sloth's fur. You've got moss in there. No wonder you're itchy. Wait right there, said Neo, which was easy for a sloth. And he ran back toward his camp. Neo came back with a long wooden spoon. "A spoon?" asked the sloth. A scratching spoon," said Neo with a grin. Neo reached up and scratched the sloth's back ever so gently. "Oh," sighed the sloth. "That's perfect." "My itch is gone," he murmured. "Now it's bedtime." Neo curled up beneath the tree, the sloth dreaming above him. The moon smiled down, and the forest sang them both to sleep.
