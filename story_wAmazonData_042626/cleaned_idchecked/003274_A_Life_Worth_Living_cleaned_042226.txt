When MJ Seide met her partner, Marty Jacobson in 1990, she found something she thought she'd never have. A family. In October 2009, MJ sat down with her 12-year-old granddaughter, Genna, at StoryCorps to talk about their family for the first time.  

GENNA ALPERIN: How has your life been different than what you thought it was gonna to be?  

MJ SEIDE: I thought that my life was probably not one that was gonna be worth living. There was this hole that I had all of my life because I never thought I'd be able to walk along the beach and hold somebody's hand, because I'm gay. But, you know, when I fell in love with Mamommy, I knew that she was my soulmate. Do you know what I mean by that?  

GENNA: Yeah.  

MJ: I'd never felt that way about anyone before. And, um, after she had gotten her divorce from Grandpa Jim, she was very upfront with your mom. She told your mom she was in love, and it was with a woman. And that was the first time that anyone was proud to say that they loved me. That made me the happiest I'd ever been in my life. And then, when I got to know your mom and Uncle Justin, I knew that I had hit paradise because I now have a family that I can wrap my arms around.  

GENNA: Is there anything that you've never told me that you wanna tell me now?
