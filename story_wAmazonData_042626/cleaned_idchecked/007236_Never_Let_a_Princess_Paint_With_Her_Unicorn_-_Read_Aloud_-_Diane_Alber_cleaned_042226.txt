I'm not sure if you noticed my dazzling crown, but I'm a princess and I do pretty much everything with my pet unicorn. So, when I heard never let a princess paint with her unicorn, I was shocked. Why would anyone say such a thing? My unicorn and I play great together. Why couldn't we paint? If I just put a little paint on a brush to test this theory out, what could go wrong? Well, I'll tell you. She licked it. Ew. Maybe that's why people think princesses shouldn't paint with their unicorns. Because unicorns will try and lick up all the paint. I had an idea. if I started to paint, she would see how much fun it was and would want to paint, too. So, I lined up all my paint cans and reminded her, we don't lick paint. But just as I started to paint, she turned around. Why didn't she want to paint with me? Turns out she just had a different way of painting. Every time she dunked her tail in the paint, it splashed more and more. "What a mess!" I yelled. Soon, my dress was dredged in paint. But we were having so much fun. I just had to see what would happen when paint touched her horn. Within seconds, it was raining, sparkling splatters. Then I heard a noise down the hall. I was going to be in so much trouble. I peeked my head out the door so my mom couldn't see my dress and I tried to explain, but before I could say anything, my eyes welled up with tears. It was no use making excuses. I'm I'm so sorry for ruining my new dress. &gt;&gt; My mom smiled. &gt;&gt; Why are you apologizing? I was hoping you would wait until your birthday, but I can see you found your new rainbow unicorn princess dress. &gt;&gt; I looked around the room and there was no paint to be found. Not on the ceiling or the walls. Then I looked down at my dress. It was the most stunning dress I have ever seen. It's hard to tell exactly why people say a princess shouldn't paint with her unicorn. It could be because it's too messy or because they think unicorns can't paint. All I know is sometimes it takes a little mess to make a magnificent masterpiece.
