When fall comes and great gaggles of Canada geese honk and flap overhead and the aspen leaves change from green to gold. Chilly air and shorter days remind us it's time to get busy. Change is coming and there's so much to do. Not just for us, but for the animals, too. Silver red salmon fight their way up rushing streams, returning to the places they were born. Chipmunks stash seeds in underground burrows. Red squirrels gather pine cones. Busy beavers dive deep, dragging woody branches to underwater piles near their lodges. In the fall, we get our house ready for cold weather. We put away patio furniture and stack wood near the back door for nights when the wind whistles. When fall comes, we hit the trail on sunny days to hike among bright yellow larches. We splash in puddles filled by heavy fall rains. And we listen for fall sounds. Elk bugling on hillsides. Squirrels rumaging for acorns in the crunchy fallen leaves. We collect driftwood on the beach and breathe in the salty air. Out in the deep sea, we know grey whales are swimming south to warmer waters. Even they are on the move. Even they know change is coming. We smile. Because it is time to get ready. There's so much to do, not just for us, but for the animals, too. When fall comes.
