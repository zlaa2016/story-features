Chen Yu often stared at them and thought, "One day I'll make a vase even finer than these. But ambition, like a fire, can burn too hot." Chen Yu nodded, but his eyes sparkled with envy. That evening, while Master Lao slept, Chen Yu crept into the workshop. He stared at the half-finished imperial vase.
