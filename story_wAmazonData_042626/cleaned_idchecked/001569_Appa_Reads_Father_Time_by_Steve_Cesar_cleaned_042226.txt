Today I've made plans, their father says. We'll grab our coats and put hats on our heads. What are we doing? Ace asks with a grim. Well, first we've got to go see your cousin. After breakfast, they get dressed and in the car to Juju's house. It isn't that far. They don't stay long. Just enough to grab Guju and go off to where the kids don't know. Their first stop is the park to play for a while. All the kids run and jump around with a smile. Dad plays with them, too, chasing them on the grass. He tries to catch them, but they move way too fast. He always played games with the kids when he could, no matter what game, even if he's no good. Ace loves that about him, so he asked that day. Why don't all parents play? I don't know, Dad says with a frown. But I do it because I don't want to let you down. I play because I love you and it's how we bond. I want us to be close forever and beyond. In the car trunk, Dad pulls out a basket of food for them to eat. He whips out a blanket and they all take a seat. Take some and pass it around. Mind your manners, please. They get hot dogs and fries. Oh, what a delicious feast. When they're full, they drive around for a while to let Baby Star rest before they drive the last miles. The third surprise is even better than before. They find their way into a little bookstore. Everyone gets to pick a book or two. Then we're off to drop off little Guju. They wonder the store and enjoy the selection before Ace asks his dad a big question. What was your favorite book as a kid? I'm not sure, Dad says. I'll have to think about it. It takes him a while before he remembers his name. Then he grabs Ace's hand and pulls him down a lane. There it is. A book with a moon on the cover. I love this more than any other. Ace grabs a book for Star to read later that day, then grabs his own books before dad goes to pay. They drop Guju off and then head back home. They've been gone all day on their fun little roam. Dad makes them supper and puts them in the bath. Ace is exhausted and ready for a nap. Mom joins them for a snuggle when she gets home that night. While Dad reads stories about a dragon and a knight. "Did you have a great day?" she asks with a smile. "It was the best day I've had in a while." "Why is that?" she asks Ace. And he knows just what to say. "Because I felt Dad's love for us all through the day." Dad smiles and hugs him. "It's definitely true. There's no one in the world that I love like you kids and Guju." They all hug again and then they say good night. Mom and dad put start to bed and turn out the lights. Ace is glad for such a great day. He loves being with his family and getting to play. As he closes his eyes, he falls asleep with a grin, remembering his day with his dad, his best friend.
