Their connection is so strong that they begin to feel emotions and sensations that they have never felt before, and they soon discover that they are swapping bodies whenever they fall asleep. At first, they try to make the best of the situation and enjoy the newfound experiences that come with being in someone else's body. However, as time goes on, they begin to feel the downsides of not being able to control their own bodies and the emotional toll it takes on them and those around them. In the meantime, the girl's grandmother becomes suspicious of the boy's presence in the house and confronts him. She threatens to call the police if he doesn't leave. So, the boy is forced to escape. He runs into a convenience store and bumps into a friend of his who invites him to go see a fireworks festival with some other friends. The boy accepts and spends the night with his friends drinking, partying, and letting loose. Meanwhile, the girl wakes up in her own body again and is relieved to be reunited with her little sister. The next morning, she finds a mysterious blue umbrella on the ground, which turns out to be a momento from the boy. She keeps the umbrella and returns to Tokyo to find the boy and give him back his body. But when she gets there, she finds that he has disappeared. All she has to show for her adventure is a blue umbrella and a handful of memories.
