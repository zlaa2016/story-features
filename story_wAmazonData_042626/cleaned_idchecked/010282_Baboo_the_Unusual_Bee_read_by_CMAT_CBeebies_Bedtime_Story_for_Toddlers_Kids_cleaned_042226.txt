There was a young bee who lived in a hive who like to dance ballet and hip hop and jive. The other bees taught him a rather strange fellow because he had pink stripes instead of bright yellow. The bee named Paboo used to sit on his own with no one to play and danced all alone. But one day, Boo felt a bit more courageous and went up to talk to a big bee, Ignatius. He quietly whispered, "Could I play with you? I'd love to be friends. My name is Baboo." Ignatius laughed loudly and answered with spite. "A pink bee that dances. It just isn't right. I'd never be friends with a bee who's so odd." Then he flicked up his wing and gave Baboo a prod. Dejected and downcast, Baboo flew away to a garden nearby, where he sat in dismay. He slumped by the stump of a juniper tree and cried as he pawed of that cruel, thoughtless bee. I wish I was yellow like all of the others. Why aren't I the same as my sisters and brothers? He looked up and saw that he'd been overheard. High up on a leaf sat a green ladybird. How come you're all green? Baboo Shily said, "All of the ladybirds I've met are red." The ladybird smiled as she flew to the tree. "Well, this is the first time I've met a pink bee." She held out her wing and said, "Le is my name. I guess being different is what makes us the same." "Well," said Baboo, "There is one thing I do whenever I'm feeling rejected or blue. If you think you can trust me, then hold out your wing, and I'll show you a magical, wonderful thing. So, they danced all night long by the light of the moon as the wind and the trees sang its beautiful tune. when all of a sudden the tiny friends heard, "Just look, a pink bee and a green ladybird." All of the insects had gathered to see the toetapping, shape-throwing Baboo and Lee. Oh, I wish I was pink. They look so unique. Their dance is delightful, so fun, and so chic. That green with that pink, what a sight to behold. A rock and roll rainbow, so bright and so bold. And even the bees from Baboo's nearby hive had come to the garden to watch the pair jive. Ignatius crept forward and bent his head low. He too had been watching their colorful show. I've made a mistake. I'm sorry, Baboo. It's just that I wish I was special like you. But you are, said Baboo. We all have our quirks. Your stinger is curly. That red really works. Your wings have a shimmer. You've got extra spots. Your shell is bright blue. You're covered in dots. I dance and they knit. You paint and she sings. Each of us looks different and likes different things. The creatures looked round and they saw it was true. Each one was as special as Lee and Baboo. As they all danced together, Baboo said, "Besides, what matters the most is who we are inside."
