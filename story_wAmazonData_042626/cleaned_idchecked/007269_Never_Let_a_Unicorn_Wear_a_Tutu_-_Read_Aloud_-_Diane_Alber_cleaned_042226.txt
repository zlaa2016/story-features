Today I overheard my friend say, "Never let a unicorn wear a tutu." Unfortunately, I didn't have a chance to ask why. Now I have this fantastic tutu for my unicorn, and I'm not sure what to do. This tutu was specifically made for a unicorn. It says so on the tag, so what could go wrong? Unicorn immediately tried it on. First, she danced. Next she twirled. After that she pranced. It was great to see her so happy. Then she did something I wasn't expecting. She took off running. Where are you going? I shouted. I soon found her at my shoe rack. Apparently a choo choo needs to be accessorized with shoes. I calmly explained to Unicorn that this will be the only time she can borrow my boots. I repeat, the only time. After I got the boots on Unicorn, she wanted me to wear some fancy boots, too. At first, I was reluctant, but I finally agreed. But then, she took off running again. This time she got into my bows. "Stop!" I yelled. After Unicorn put bows in her mane and tail, she wanted me to wear bows, too. And once again, she took off running. "Where are you going now?" I hollered. "No, not the jewelry." I screeched, but it was too late. Necklaces, rings, and bracelets were flying everywhere. After Unicorn layered herself in jewelry, she wanted me to wear jewelry, too. But then she took off running again. Unicorn headed right for the kitchen and started tossing cupcakes at me. Miraculously, all the cupcakes landed perfectly on the plate. I thought Unicorn would be ecstatic. I had saved all the cupcakes, but instead a little tearrop down her cheek. She looked so upset. I tried to figure out what happened, but I couldn't understand what Unicorn was trying to say. I started to think, if I were a unicorn with an amazing tutu, fancy boots, sparkling bows, dazzling jewelry, and yummy cupcakes. Why would I be sad? I soon realized that there was one thing missing. I quickly rushed to my closet. I began rumaging through my clothes and I pulled out a tutu and announced twinsies. Unicorn jumped for joy. Unicorn made me feel special all day. She included me from the minute she tried on the tutu, the fancy boots, the hair bows, the dazzling jewelry. She even invited me to eat the yummy cupcakes. It all makes sense now. Never let a unicorn wear a tutu unless you're wearing a matching one, too. The end.
