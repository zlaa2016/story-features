In a green meadow surrounded by tall oak trees lived a strong brown horse named Hero. He had a shiny mane, powerful legs, and kind eyes that sparkled in the sunlight. Hero could run faster than the wind. He could pull heavy carts, carry the farmer through muddy fields, and even help build wooden barns by dragging big logs. Everyone said, "There's no horse like Hero." But Hero wasn't happy. Every day he looked up at the sky and watched the birds soar and dance in the clouds. "How lucky they are!" he sighed. "If only I had wings, I could fly, too." At night, Hero dreamed of flying. He imagined spreading shiny golden wings and gliding over mountains and rivers. He saw himself as the flying king of horses. Then one magical night, Hero's dream came true. He opened his eyes and gasped. Wings had grown from his back. He nighed with joy and leapt into the sky. At first, it was amazing. The wind rushed through his mane. He felt free, powerful, and proud. "I can go anywhere. I'm better than every horse on the ground," he shouted. But soon things started to go wrong. His big hooves broke the tops of trees. He scared the birds from their nests. When he tried to land, he smashed a fence and frightened the farm animals. The cows mooded in fear. The sheep ran in all directions. Even the farmer's children cried, "Hero, stop! You're hurting everything!" Hero looked around. The farm was a mess. His wings drooped. "What have I done?" he whispered sadly. The gentle wind blew past him and whispered, "Hero, you were made to run, not to fly." The wind swirled around him. The birds have their wings, and you have your strong legs. You bring the farmer his crops. You give the children happy rides. And your gallop sounds like the heartbeat of the meadow. That is your own special kind of magic. Hero thought about the farmer's smile and the children's laughter. He didn't need to be a bird to be wonderful. He just needed to be Hero. He closed his eyes and wished with all his heart to be himself again. When he opened his eyes, the heavy wings were gone. He felt light and strong. He kicked up his heels and galloped across the fields faster than ever before. He was happy just being a horse. Hero learned that every creature has its own special gift. The birds have the sky and he had the beautiful wide open meadow. And being the best horse he could be was the most wonderful magic of all.
