In a quiet fishing village by the sea, there lived a boy named Keon. He was known for one thing. He always asked questions. Why is the sea blue? Why do fish sparkle? Why does thunder sound angry? The villagers laughed kindly. Keon, you ask too many questions. But Keon didn't stop. He loved wondering why. One morning, the sea turned rough and dark. The fishermen gathered, worried. The winds are strange, said one. Maybe it's a sea monster. Keon frowned. Could it really be a monster? Or maybe something else? He wanted to know the truth, not just the stories people told. So, with his grandfather's old compass and a tiny boat, Keon decided to find out. Keon sailed into the unknown sea. The sky grew heavy, the waves rose high, but his heart stayed brave. He whispered to himself, "If I never question, I'll never understand." Hours later, he saw something huge moving beneath the water. Not a monster, but a blue whale. The whale rose beside his boat, calm and gentle. Little one, it said in a voice like thunder and song. Why are you here? Keon replied in awe. I wanted to know why the sea changed color and grew wild. The whale spoke in a kind tone. Because the moon pulls the water. Because the wind moves the tides. The sea listens to the sky. Not monsters or magic.
