The Rainbow Fish was the most beautiful fish in the sea, but he never played with other fish. I'm too beautiful, he thought. Once a little blue fish asked for one of his shining scales, but the rainbow fish just left. Never, he said. After that, the other fish swam away from him. The rainbow fish was all alone. He went to the starfish for help. "Why doesn't anybody like me?" he asked. "Go ask the octopus," the starfish said. "She is very wise." The octopus said, "Give away your shiny scales.
