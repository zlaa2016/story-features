In a world filled with materialism, negativity, and egotism, one man has created an empire while focusing on a message of hard work, empathy, and positivity. And now, this man has turned his attention to the most important segment of our society, our children. Serial entrepreneur, chairman of multiple businesses, and sixtime New York time best-selling author Gary Veaynerchuk brings to you V. V Friends is a contemporary entertainment company that combines art, storytelling, collecting, events, and innovative technologies to build a community around a movement of kindness and positivity. Each of the 283 V friends has their own attribute designed to teach children different values. What has us at Book League Storytime Adventures the most excited though is the brand new book by Gary Veaynerchuk called Meet Me in the Middle, which tells two stories from different points of view, featuring Patient Pig and Eager Eagle.
