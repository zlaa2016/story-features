Elmo gives thanks, words by Aaron Ginsburgger, and pictures by Ernie Quat. There's so much to be thankful for. Where do you start? Everyday gratitude comes from the heart. We're grateful for our bodies that work hard each day that help us to move, to grow, and to play. We give thanks for good food that fills up our tummy, and cooking together to make meals that are yummy. For sun in the summer and leaves in the fall, winter snow and spring flowers. We're glad for them all. for time spent exploring the world all around. We're thankful for all the adventures we found. We're grateful for giggles and silly things, too. Smiling brings joy to me and to you. We're thankful for kindness, for helping and caring for those who show love by their giving and sharing to. We love to create, to imagine, and play. We're thankful we think of new ideas each day. We're grateful for learning for teachers and schools. Our knowledge and wisdom are powerful tools. There's so much we share and we're each special. It's true. Our differences make the whole world special, too. Sometimes we make mistakes, some big and some small. We're proud of ourselves and celebrate it all. We're grateful for time spent with family and friends. We cherish these moments from beginning to end. We're thankful for love that fills up our hearts that we give and receive even when we're apart. For a space that feels safe in a spot of our own. We're thankful for that special place we call home. We treasure all of these things and so many more. All around us, there's so much to be thankful for.
