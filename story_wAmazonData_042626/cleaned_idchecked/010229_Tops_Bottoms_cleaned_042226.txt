Once upon a time, there lived a very lazy bear who had lots of money and lots of land. His father had been a hard worker and a smart business bear, and he had given all of his wealth to his son. But all bear wanted to do was sleep. Not far down the road lived a hair. Although hair was clever, he sometimes got into trouble. He had once owned land, too. But now he had nothing. He had lost a risky bet with a tortoise and had sold all of his land to bear to pay off the debt. "Hair and his family were in very bad shape." "The children are so hungry, Father Hair. We must think of something," Mrs. hair cried one day. So hair and Mrs. Hair put their heads together and cooked up a plan. The next day, Hair hopped down the road to Bear's house. Bear, of course, was asleep. Hello, Bear. Wake up. It's your neighbor, Hair, and I have an idea. Bear opened one eye and grunted. We can be business partners, Hair said. All we need is this field right here in front of your house. I'll do the hard work of planting and harvesting, and we can split the profit right down the middle. Yes, sir. Bear, we're in this together. I'll work and you sleep. Huh? said Bear. So, what will it be? Bear asked Hair. The top half or the bottom half. It's up to you. Tops or bottoms? Let's see. Bear said with a yawn. I'll take the top half. Hair. Right. Tops. Hair smiled. It's a done deal, Bear. So Bear went back to sleep and hair and his family went to work. Hair planted, Mrs. Hair watered, and everyone weeded. Bear slept as the crops grew. When it was time for the harvest, hair called out, "Wake up, Bear. You get the tops and I get the bottoms. Hair and his family dug up the carrots, the radishes, and the beets. Hair plucked off all the tops, tossed them into a pile for Bear, and put the bottoms aside for himself. Bear stared at his pile. "But hair, all the best parts are in your half. You chose the tops, bear, hair said. Now, hair, you've tricked me. You plant this field again, and this season I want the bottoms. Hair agreed. It's a done deal, Bear. So, Bear went back to sleep. And Hair and his family went to work. They planted, watered, and weeded. Bear slept as the crops grew. When it was time for the harvest, Hair called out, "Wake up, Bear. You get the bottoms and I get the tops." Hair and his family gathered up the lettuce, the broccoli, and the celery. Hair pulled off the bottoms for Bear and put the tops in his own pile. Bear looked at his pile and scrawled. Hair, you have cheated me again. But Bear, Hair said, you wanted the bottoms this time. Bear growled. You plant this field again, Hair. You've tricked me twice, and you owe me one season of both tops and bottoms. You're right, poor old bear, sighed hair. It's only fair that you get both tops and bottoms this time. It's a done deal, Bear. So, Bear went back to sleep and Hair and his family went to work. They planted, watered, and weeded, then watered and weeded some more. Bear slept as the crops grew. When it was time for the harvest, hair called out, "Wake up, Bear. This time you get the tops and the bottoms." There, in front of Bear's house lay a high field of corn. Hair and his family yanked up every cornstck. Hair tugged off the roots at the bottom and the tassels at the top and put them in a pile for bear. Then he carefully collected the ears of corn in the middle and placed them in his own pile. Bear rubbed his eyes and watched. See bear, you get the tops and the bottoms. I get the middles. Yes, sir. Bear, it's a done deal. By now, Bear was wide awake. That's it. Hair, he hollered. From now on, I'll plant my own crops and take the tops, bottoms, and middles. Hair and his family scooped up the corn and hopped down the road toward home. Bear never again slept through a season of planting and harvesting. Hair bought back his land with the profit from the crops, and he and Mrs. Hair opened a vegetable stand. And although hair and bear learned to live happily as neighbors, they never became business partners again. The bear decided to stop sleeping so much so that he could plant and garden and have food to eat. and the rabbit lived next door with all his family and it all worked out really well.
