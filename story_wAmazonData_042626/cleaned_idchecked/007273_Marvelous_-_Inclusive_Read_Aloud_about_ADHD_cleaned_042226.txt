Marcus loved to build. Every material was an invitation to explore and every day was an opportunity to create. I built another marvel, he exclaimed. But sometimes building houses, towers, and bridges got in the way of everything else. When his teacher, Miss Anderson, asked his class to write a story, Marcus was distracted by his pencils and built a rocket ship. When he was asked to write spelling words, he picked up paper and constructed a robot. When he was assigned math problems, he built crayon houses instead of number sentences. When Miss Anderson asked the class to create a blueprint design for a new school playground, Marcus was ready to try his best. As he pulled out his pencil, the blocks in the corner caught his attention. Suddenly, his busy brain shifted. He got out of his chair. A builder on a mission. He was inspired. "Marcus, time to focus on your blueprint," Miss Anderson reminded him gently. I will. But did you see my marvel? Marcus replied. Marcus tried again and again to focus on the task. But every time a material caught his eye. It was hard to sit still. It was hard to focus. When it was time to play soccer outside, Marcus built a tower. When it was time to borrow books from the library, he built a bridge. When it was time to play tag at recess, Marcus built a zipline. Soon enough, the entire school became his design lab. He tinkered. He experimented. He failed. He had new ideas. He tried again. Finally, he was ready. On the day of the presentations, students shared their blueprints. Each one showed extravagant playgrounds filled with extraordinary structures. When it was his turn, Marcus stood proudly at the front of the class and shared his project. Jasmine raised her hand. I thought we had to draw a blueprint. Marcus didn't do that. Marcus looked at his creation. This is how my brain saw the playground. This is how I imagined my design. Even though it was different from what the other students had done, it was exactly perfect for him. Everyone in the class admired his playground. His creation was beyond anyone's imagination. It was truly a marvel. And everyone gazed at the marvel in front of them. Remember, everyone's brain works differently. Everyone is unique and you are all perfect.
