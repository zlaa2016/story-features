In your dreams, the sun had gone down, the moon in the sky. It was now time for bed. No more questions. Why? Alexander crawled under his blankets and laid down his head. His mama turned off the lights and his nightlight turned red. This meant it was time to calm down and sleep. But this was always so hard. Stay in bed. Be quiet. Don't make a peep. He snuggled his trucks and closed his eyes. But he just didn't feel tired. What a surprise. His mama tucked him up all nice and snug. Please, one more book. One more kiss. One more hug. Mama came in calmly for that last big embrace, gave him a squeeze, and nuzzled his face. She could tell he was scared. In his eyes there was fright. So she leaned in and whispered, "What should we do in your dreams tonight?" He suddenly got quiet, thinking thought after thought. How could he pick one when he had quite a lot?
