Ever heard of a boy raised by wolves? No, I'm not kidding. This is the story of Mali, a man cub growing up in the heart of the Indian jungle. His best friends, a wise black panther named Bagira and a fun-loving, carefree bear named Belaloo. For mo life is just one big adventure swinging on vines, swimming with his furry pals and learning the bare necessities of life from Bulu. But the jungle has its dangers and the fearsome tiger Shir Khan has returned. Bagira and Baloo no Mowgli isn't safe anymore. They have to convince him to leave his only home and go to the man village. But how do you tell a boy who thinks he's a bear that he belongs with humans? It's a tough, heartwarming, and sometimes hilarious journey to figure out where you truly belong.
