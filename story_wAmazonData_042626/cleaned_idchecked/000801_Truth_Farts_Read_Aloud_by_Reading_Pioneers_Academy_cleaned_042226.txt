Billy had been warned along the way, that truth farts could show up on any given day, but he never believed what they had to say. That is until one day. Let's rewind and go over that day. It started out with only a sliver. Xext thing he knew he heard his mom say, "look me in the eye and tell me did you eat the blueberry pie? Whatever you do just, please don't lie." Billy had to think quickly he only had a few seconds before he had to tell his story. He felt his stomach churn and he started to worry. The pie had been left in plain view, it could have been a pigeon or two. It could have been any creature, maybe a mouse or even a beaver. Billy could blame it on a bear, but that wouldn't be fair. Then he thought, whatever I don't care I'll say anything. I need to so I don't get in trouble. Then all of a sudden he felt a big bubble. He started to scramble, he started to ramble, he had so many stories which one would he choose. He couldn't look his mother in the eye. He could feel himself starting, starting to lie. He told his mom he saw a pigeon pick at it. He told his mom he saw a beaver take a hack at it. He told his mom he saw a mouse carry a little bit back to her tiny house. He even told his mom about the big grizzly bear. Billy had told so many lies he could see the hurt forming in his mother's eyes. Then suddenly out of the blue, something else had entered the room. Oh phew! It was just a little fart, but oh no the little fart smelled like something right from the start. Poor little guy, everyone knows truth farts never lie. And this one smelled just like... blueberry pie! I guess you could say the rest is history. The truth fart had solved the big pie mystery. The whole situation wasn't so pretty for our little friend Billy. In the end, Billy felt a sense of relief. He didn't like lying and feeling so much grief. He told his mom he was sorry for his lies. Billy realized he should never lie so he didn't even try. There was no point. everyone knows Truth farts never lie!
