High in the eucalyptus trees of the Australian bush, a little grey koala named Kylie was just waking from her long afternoon nap. She yawned as she stretched her fluffy ears, rubbed her sleepy eyes, and blinked up at the darkening sky. The first stars were beginning to twinkle above the gum leaves. Kylie loved this time of the night. The air was cool, the bush was quiet, and the stars always made her feel calm. But tonight, something seemed... different. Kylie tilted her head. "One star is missing," she whispered softly. "The bright one that always shines above my tree isn't there". Kylie wasn't afraid, but she was curious. She decided to go looking. As she climbed carefully down her tree, she saw her best friend, Wally the Wombat, shuffling nearby. Wally was carrying a small lantern, his round nose twitching. "Evening, Kylie," Wally said kindly. "Why are you out so late?" "I think a star is missing". Kylie explained. "The bright one I always see before bed. I want to find out where it went". Wally scratched his head. "I don't know much about stars, but I'll come with you. Friends stick together". So side by side, Kylie and Wally paddled through the bush. The night was gentle. The crickets chirped softly and a cool breeze rustled the leaves. Before long, they met Penny the platypus, who was splashing quietly at the river's edge. "Kylie, Wally, what are you doing here so late?" she asked, shaking water from her bill. Kylie told her about the missing star. Penny thought for a moment, then smiled. "The river. The river reflects the whole sky. Maybe if we look together, we'll see something". The three friends peered into the calm silvery water. The stars sparkled back at them like diamonds, but the bright star Kylie loved wasn't there. Kylie sighed. "I miss it. I always look out for that star before bedtime. Without it, the sky the sky feels empty". Wally placed a gentle paw on her shoulder. "Don't worry, Kylie. Even if one star is gone, you still have us". Penny nodded. "That's right. Stars are beautiful, but friends friends shine just as brightly". Just then, a cheerful laugh echoed through the trees. It was Kevin the Kookaburra perched on a high branch. "Looking for a star? Are you?" Kevin chuckled. "Sometimes clouds hide them away, but they always come back". Kylie looked up and sure enough a soft cloud was drifting across the sky. Slowly it passed and there, right above her tree, was the missing star, twinkling brightly again. Kylie's heart warmed. "There it is, my special star". Her friends smiled. They had walked together, searched together, and reminded her that she was never alone. Wally yawned. "It's late. Time for all of us to go to bed". So, Kylie climbed back up her gum tree, hugged the trunk with her little paws. Wally waddled back to his burrow, and Penny slipped quietly into her riverbank den, and Kevin fluffed his feathers and closed his eyes. As Kylie curled into a soft ball, she gazed one last time at her shining star. But now she felt something even brighter. The glow of her friendships. As the stars twinkled over the gum trees, Kylie the koala drifted into the sweetest dream, knowing that she was never alone. The end.
