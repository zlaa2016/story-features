Four big cars, orange, yellow, green, and blue. Said orange long and low. Vroom, said yellow, ready to go. Gr said green big and mean. Roar, roar, the blue car said. Wait, there's one more. It was Little Red. "Putt," said Little Red. The starter's flag waved and off they sped. "Roar! Vroom!" When the dust had cleared, there was red. "Putt said Little Red." Over, under, around, and through went orange, yellow, green, and blue. Up and down, down and around through the town and country scene went yellow, orange, blue, and green. Those other drivers laughed at Red. They all said that Red's a joke. He's nothing but an old slowpoke. What? said Red. He didn't say gr or vroom or roar. Little Red said what he said before. Putt, putt, putt, putt, putt, putt. There goes yellow taking the lead. Green is second, putting on lead. Orange and blue catching up fast. Where was red? Dead last. I cannot lose, shouted yellow. A very boastful sort of fellow. A first place finish is my goal. But he was so busy bragging. He did not see a big pothole. Said the others roaring past. Where was Red? Still behind, but no longer last. Who will win the big race? Can these cars keep up the pace? Up ahead, Dead Bear's Curve. I won't slow down for Dead Bear's curve. Orange's driver was all nerve. All nerve, but not much sense. Orange went right through the fence. It's a twocar race, Green's driver said, forgetting all about Little Red. "Putt," said Little Red. The driver of green was really mean. He reached into the glove compartment. It was time for the dirty tricks department. It wasn't a very nice thing to do, but that was that. The blue car's tires all went flat. I've got it one. Green driver said the finish line is just ahead. As he said it, a fast food store came into sight. H said green. Racing gives me an appetite. I have beaten orange, yellow, and blue. As for Red, nowhere in view. There's plenty of time for a little snack, a burger, and fries, please, in a sack. But Red never quit. He just kept on coming. His putt putt motor kept right on humming. And while Green waited for his stuff, Red putted right past. The fast food store wasn't fast enough. Look, look, it's Little Red. Hooray! Hooray! The bears all said the road race winner is Little Red. Putt, putt, putt, said little red.
