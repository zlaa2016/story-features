The Donkey's House. One sunny morning, a clever young fox named Felix found a note tucked into his mailbox. It was an invitation written in loopy, friendly handwriting. You are invited to tea, it read, "At the new home of your friend, Danny the donkey." Felix smiled. "A new house and tea with a friend sounded like a perfect adventure." Felix brushed his red fur until it shone and set off across the meadow. The sun was warm and wild flowers bobbed in the gentle breeze. He hummed a happy tune, imagining what a donkey's house might look like. Would it be big? Would it be made of sticks? He couldn't wait to see. At the edge of the woods, he found it. A cozy little cottage with a sturdy wooden door. Danny the donkey stood proudly in the doorway, his big, friendly face beaming. Welcome, Felix," he braided cheerfully. "I'm so glad you could come.
