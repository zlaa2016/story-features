Little ghost loves every inch of his neighborhood, but most of the year he stays home. He's afraid he'll scare his neighbors. At Halloween, though, Little Ghost blends right in. Little ghost and his mom have been haunting their creaky old house, just the two of them, for a hundred years. They've seen many neighbors come and go. But he's never once felt brave enough to say hello. Mom, look. New neighbors. Little Ghost notices lots of things he and the new girl have in common. This time he decides, I will do it.
