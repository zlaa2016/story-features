Aly Max lightning has finally qualified for gamer a championship where players compete to be Top Dog in the multiplayer video game Lone Wolf of Calamity Bay but his dreams of domination are quickly dashed when his parents sent him to Camp reset a tech-free summer camp instead long nights of energy drinks and Screen Time have been replaced with fresh air and group activities under the hot sun a shock to the system for a lone wolf like Max can Max Escape Camp reset and level up at gamer or has he finally played his last match
