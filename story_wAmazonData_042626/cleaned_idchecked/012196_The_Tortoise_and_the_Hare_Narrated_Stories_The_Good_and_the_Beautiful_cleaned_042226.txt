Once there was a little tortoise. He lived in a beautiful wooded area which was just right for him. Leaves and branches fell often, covering the ground. The tortoise shuffled through the leaves often, eating his fill and exploring his home. It was almost perfect for the little tortoise. One thing about the woods was not perfect, though. A hare lived in the woods too, and he always ran fast. The hare didn't like to stop for anything. Some hares went slowly sometimes, looking at a flower here or nibbling at a leafy green plant there. This hare did not go slow, though. He always ran fast. Whenever the tortoise was in his way, the hare would zoom right by him! Sometimes he almost knocked the tortoise down. The tortoise went slowly, never running fast like the hare. He picked at the leaves near his feet. He walked into sunny spots and stayed there, enjoying the warmth. Sometimes he stayed in the sun for a very long time. Sometimes, he stayed there all day. He didn't want to move quickly. He didn't need to move quickly. So, he went slowly and that was perfect for him. A trail led through the woods. It cut between the trees and showed where animals had traveled many times. Sometimes, the tortoise liked to walk along this trail. The tall trees were thinner there, so more sunlight could reach the ground. The tortoise liked this, but the hare liked that trail too. He liked to run there. That was where he ran his fastest. One day, the hare saw the tortoise on the trail. The hare raced to the tortoise, zooming on by with a great big whoosh, saying, "Let's have a race!" The tortoise kept on walking down the trail. He was going the same way as the hare, but he didn't need to go fast. After running far, the hare stopped. He cuddled up under a cozy tree and dozed while the sun shined and birds chirped all around him. He didn't need to run fast when the tortoise was so slow. He would take a nice, long rest. While the hare lay under the cozy tree, the tortoise kept on walking. After a long time, he saw the sleeping hare, but he didn't stop. He kept on walking. There was a nice pond at the end of the trail and that's where the tortoise was headed. He wanted to go for a swim! Slowly but surely, he kept on walking, never forgetting where he was going. All day long the tortoise stuck to his goal. Finally, he was close to his destination. As the sun began to set, the hare began to wake. Soon he remembered the trail and the tortoise, and he wanted to run again. Barely awake, the hare took off, zooming away as quickly as he could. The hare had slept the day away though, so the tortoise reached the end of the trail first and won the race. With a satisfying cooling feeling, the pond's waters touched the tortoise's feet. In he walked, ready for a nice long swim. But then, splash! The hare dashed into the pond, sending water flying everywhere. The tortoise watched. The hare splashed, then the hare bounded away again. For a moment, the pond's water swirled and sloshed, but soon all was calm again. The tortoise continued his swim. He saw some fish, he ate some leaves, and when he was done with his swim, he turned toward home again. He liked being slow and steady. It was just right for him. He wasn't the fastest, but he had reached his destination and he was happy.
