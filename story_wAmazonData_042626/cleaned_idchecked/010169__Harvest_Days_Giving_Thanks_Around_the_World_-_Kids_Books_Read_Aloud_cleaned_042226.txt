People as far back as anyone's known have gathered to give thanks for the food we have grown. A bountiful harvest is perfect to share. What a wonderful way to show others you care. South Korea. When summer is ending and leaves will fall soon, we gather for chok beneath a full moon. We roll sticky son pion together with speed. We all love the ones stuffed with sesame seeds. Poland. When doki comes, we cut the last sheath. We tie it with flowers and make a big wreath. We offer the grains and the loaves of fresh bread. Giving thanks we have food for the cold days ahead. Tamil Nadu, India. On the third day of Pongal, we thank all the cows and the powerful bulls who work hard pulling plows. We dress up the cattle and hand feed them treats, the pots boiling over with sweet rice to eat. Bolivia Martesaya is quite a display. We thank the Earth Mother on Carnival Day. We offer up water as fireworks crack. Our planet provides. So we want to give back. Ghana at Homo. Each year the GA people recall dry days in the past when the harvest was small. Now laughing at hunger, we all dance and sing. We're thankful for rain and the harvest it brings. Barbados. Our ancestors side with relief in the sun. Once the backbreaking work of the harvest was done, when crop over comes, we wear feathers and crowns. We take pride in our heritage, passing it down. Morocco. When it's time for Sukkot, the building we make is nice to spend time in. Asleep or awake, our family and friends come to meet us inside, grateful for all that Hashem can provide. Liberia, each year in November comes Thanksgiving Day. We show that we're grateful in all different ways. Some bring fruit to the church and then hear pastors preach and others enjoy a fun day at the beach. Iran. Our mehon table has flowers and bread, some candles, a mir, and fruit that is red. We joyfully toss herbs and seeds in the air, embracing to show one another we care. Spain, it's laina. Tomatoes are ripe. Let's head to the streets for a rowdy food fight. When tomatoes start flying, you all better run. But don't throw them too hard. It's only for fun. Punjab, India. We harvest the wheat when we feel fresh spring air. Visaki is here and it's time for the fair. The mela puts us in a jovial mood. We love all the dancing, the rides, and the good food. Germany. Every St. Martin's day brings a parade. We march through the streets with the lanterns we've made. We all sing together by candlelight glow. Stars twinkle above and we twinkle below. People have changed how we live through the years, but we still come together when harvest time near. We give thanks for our earth and the hard work that's done to grow the world's harvest to feed everyone.
