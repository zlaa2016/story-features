A day on the river. Zach and his cat Sydney loved to explore the river in his canoe. His grandfather had built the canoe and Zach had inherited it from him. It was hollowed out from a big tree. They passed through a beautiful village. "Hello, little guy," said Zach. Why are you sat here all alone? Where is your mother and your brothers and sisters? The baby duck hopped into the canoe and sat between Zach and his cat. I think he wants some company, Sydney. Maybe we will see his family along the river somewhere. They paddled back up the river toward the bridge again. Keep your eyes open, Sydney, said Zach. His family must be around here somewhere. The little duck sat next to Sydney and watched the swans float past. As they floated under the bridge, they saw a mother goose with her babies. "That's not your mommy," said Zach to the baby duck. "Don't worry, we will find her." Look, there is your mommy and your brothers and sisters. We found them. The baby duck flapped his little wings with excitement. The baby duck was so happy to find his family, he gave Zach the biggest hug he could manage to say thank you. Then he jumped into the water and swam to meet his mother, who was overjoyed to see her little lost one. "What a great day we had on the river, Sydney," said Zach. "I'm so glad we could help that little duck find his family. It feels good to help others." Sydney purred and smiled in agreement.
