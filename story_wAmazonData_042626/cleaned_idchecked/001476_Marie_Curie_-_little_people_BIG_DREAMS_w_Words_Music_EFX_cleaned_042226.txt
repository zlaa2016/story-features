marie curry marie curry was born maria salamia sladdowska in warsaw poland she is most famous for winning two nobel prizes one for physics and one for chemistry she was the first female scientist to win a nobel prize and the only female so far to win two in different subjects she was a remarkable child with an incredible love of learning she overcame the loss of her mother at the age of 10 to become a brilliant student despite maria's talents she wasn't allowed to go to the same university as her brother because she was a girl she worked as a teacher and a governess before moving to paris to study where she became marie it was at this time that she met pierre curry they later married and together made the great scientific discovery of polonium and radium and in 1903 they won the nobel prize for physics after the sudden loss of pierre who was killed in a street accident marie threw herself into work she won a second nobel prize this time for chemistry founded the radium institute at the university of paris and developed the use of x-rays to help injured soldiers in the first world war marie believed in the beauty of science and that people could use science for good not evil she used all of her skills to search for knowledge and her discoveries continue to help people with illnesses today
