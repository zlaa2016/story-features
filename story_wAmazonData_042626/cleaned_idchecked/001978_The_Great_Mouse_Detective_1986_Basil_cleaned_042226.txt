In the fog draped heart of Victorian London. Beneath the feet of bustling crowds and the flicker of gas lamps, lies a secret world. A world built to a smaller scale where mice and rats live out adventures just as grand as our own. This is the story of Basil of Baker Street, the cleverest mouse detective in all of England and his ultimate showdown with the Napoleon of crime, the wicked genius known as Professor Ratigan. Our tale begins on a stormy night at a quaint toy shop. The kind and gifted toy maker, Hyram Fluffer Sham, is celebrating his daughter, Olivia's birthday. Suddenly, the door crashes open. A sinister bat with a peg leg. A goon named Fidget swoops in and kidnaps Hyram, leaving poor Olivia all alone in the rain. Meanwhile, Dr. David Kudosen, a brave mouse army surgeon just returned from service in Afghanistan, is wandering through the fog. He finds Olivia crying and promising to help takes her to the only one who can solve such a mystery. 221 and a half Baker Street, the home of the brilliant but eccentric detective Basil. At first, Basil is lost in his experiments, but when Olivia mentions the bat with a peg leg, his ears perk up. This is the clue he's been waiting for. Fidget works for none other than Professor Ratigan, Basil's archeneemy, a criminal mastermind with a plan to rule all of M. While Basil dives into the case, let's take a peek into Ratigan's shadowy world. Deep in the sewers, Ratigan is holding court with his gang of thugs. He's a rat, but he absolutely insists on being called a large mouse. Anyone who dares to call him a rat gets a one-way ticket to his pet cat, Felicia. Ratigan unveils his diabolical plan. He's forced the captured toy maker to build a mechanical replica of the mouse queen. He'll use this robot to take over the kingdom and crown himself the supreme ruler of all mice. The stage is set for his most evil scheme yet. Back on the case, Basil, Dawson, and Olivia race into the night. They follow Fidget's trail to a toy shop where they find a list of stolen items, gears, tools, and uniforms, all pointing toward Ratigan's plot. But just as they piece it together, Fidget ambushes them, snatches Olivia, and disappears into the darkness. Devastated, but more determined than ever, Basil and Dawson retreat to Basil's lab. Using chemistry, deduction, and a bit of a flare for the dramatic, Basil pinpoints Ratagon's hideout in the sewers beneath the river temps. They disguise themselves as sailors and sneak into a rowdy tavern called the Rat Trap, filled with the shadyiest rodents in London. After a wild and chaotic musical number, their cover is blown and they are captured by Ratigan himself. Gloating in his victory, Ratigan reveals his grand plan will come to fruition that very night. During the queen's diamond jubilee celebration, he ties Basil and Dawson to a ridiculously elaborate death trap made of mouse traps, crossbows, and gears, all time to go off when a record finishes playing. As Ratigan leaves them to their fate, Datson is filled with despair. But Basil, the master of deduction, never gives up. He studies the intricate mechanism, his brilliant mind racing just as the final note of the record plays. He calculates the perfect moment to trigger a chain reaction. With flawless timing, he sets off a cascade that destroys the trap and sets them both free. Reinvigorated, Basil dusts himself off and exclaims, "The games a foot." The action shifts to Buckingham Palisaur. Rather, the miniature version hidden right beneath it. Ratigan's mechanical double of the queen is unveiled. The robot queen denounces the real one and proclaims Ratagon as the new ruler. But just as the villain is about to savor his victory. Basil, Dawson, and Olivia burst onto the scene. A thrilling chase erupts through the palace. Gears fly, springs pop, and toy soldiers collide in glorious chaos. Olivia bravely takes on Fidget, helping to expose Ratigan's plot to the entire court. In a brilliant move, Basil seizes control of the mechanical queen and uses it to turn Ratigan's own guards against him. The real queen is saved, but Ratigan, furious, grabs Olivia and makes his escape. The chase leads them into the towering clockwork of Big Ben for one of Disney's most dramatic showdowns. Inside the massive ticking gears, Basil and Ratigan battle it out among grinding cogs and swinging pendulums. Rain lashes the metal frame as thunder roars across the city. Basil manages to save Olivia, getting her to safety, but Ratigan, now transformed into a feral beast of pure rage, lunges at him. The two rivals fall onto the clock's massive hands, dangling precariously high above the city streets. As the clock strikes midnight, the final deafening chime sends Ratigan plummeting into the fog below. Defeated at last, Basil clings desperately to a gear, pulling himself to safety. The grateful toy maker thanks our heroes and Olivia gives Basil a heartfelt hug before they depart. Inspired by their incredible adventure, Dr. Dson decides to stay in London and become Basil's permanent partner, his very own Watson.
